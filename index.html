🛡️ RDL Fair Play Policy

🤝 Respect All Players

  • Treat everyone with respect before, during, and after matches.
  • Friendly banter is allowed, but harassment, bullying, or targeted insults are not.
  • No discrimination, hate speech, or offensive slurs of any kind.

💬 Toxic Behavior

The following is prohibited:

  • Excessive trash talk intended to provoke or harass.
  • Personal attacks or insults.
  • Threats or encouraging others to target a player.
  • Spamming, flaming, or intentionally creating drama.
  • Witch-hunting or public callouts without evidence.
  • Overuse of toxic emotes (example: Take the L).

🏆 Good Sportsmanship

  • Win with humility.
  • Lose respectfully.
  • Shake off disagreements without escalating arguments.
  • Respect referee and staff decisions while using the proper appeal process if necessary.

🚫 Unsportsmanlike Conduct

  • Intentionally disrupting matches.
  • Delaying games without a valid reason.
  • Refusing to cooperate with league staff.
  • Attempting to provoke opponents into breaking rules.
  • Encouraging teammates or fans to harass others.

📢 Reporting Conduct

  • Report rule violations privately to league staff.
  • Provide screenshots, videos, or other evidence whenever possible.
  • False reports or edited evidence may result in penalties.

⚖️ Disciplinary Actions

  • Warning
  • Match mute
  • Temporary suspension
  • Match forfeiture (if behavior affects competition)
  • Season suspension
  • Permanent league ban

👤 Team Responsibility

  • Team captains are responsible for helping maintain good conduct within their roster.
  • Repeated misconduct by multiple members of the same team may result in additional team penalties.

📜 Final Note

RDL is committed to providing a competitive, respectful, and enjoyable environment for all players. Toxicity, harassment, and unsportsmanlike behavior will not be tolerated. League staff reserve the right to investigate incidents and issue appropriate penalties to preserve competitive integrity.
